How to Play

Explore Brno in Singleplayer

While the public server is no longer live, you can easily load the full 13 GB world archive into your own Minecraft and explore the city alone or with friends.

1

1. Download the Map

Choose between the pristine algorithmic generation or the final server snapshot with player creations.

2

2. Extract to 'saves'

Unzip the downloaded archive directly into your Minecraft saves directory.

3

3. Launch Java 1.19.2

Start Minecraft Java Edition version 1.19.2 and select Brno in your Singleplayer world list.

Project Origin

How It All Started

The original ambition was simply to model the Faculty of Informatics at Masaryk University for virtual open days during the COVID-19 pandemic. The area around the faculty felt a bit empty, so we decided to add a few neighboring buildings... and the project quickly grew. During the #HackujBrno hackathon, we succeeded in generating the entire city of Brno into Minecraft. With backing from the City of Brno and data.Brno, we operated a public server for players worldwide.

Technology

How It Works Under the Hood

In short: the entire city is generated algorithmically. We use a high-resolution GIS database populated with open city data alongside custom procedural generation algorithms.

Custom World Generator

The heart of the system is our custom Generator plugin, which replaces the default Minecraft world generator. Connected to an 8 GB geodatabase combining data.Brno and OpenStreetMap, it translates building footprints, heights, roof types, and street layouts into Minecraft blocks. Instead of manual building, a small tweak in code transforms how entire districts materialize.

20×20 km Pregenerated World

The pregenerated Brno world spans approximately 20×20 km (about 10,000 blocks in every direction from the city center) and weighs 13 GB. Generating the map took nearly a full week of non-stop computation—especially due to the complex terrain around the Brno Reservoir and surrounding forest hills.

Runtime Plugin & Easter Eggs

In addition to the world generator, we built a custom Runtime plugin to handle treasure placement, multi-world transitions, and various hidden Brno Easter Eggs.
