Installation Guide

Step-by-Step Singleplayer Installation Guide

Installing the world archive into your Minecraft is straightforward. Follow these steps:

1

Step 1: Download the ZIP archive

Click either of the download links above to save the world archive to your computer.

2

Step 2: Locate your 'saves' folder

Open your Minecraft saves directory depending on your operating system:

Windows: %appdata%\.minecraft\saves
macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves
Linux: ~/.minecraft/saves
3

Step 3: Extract the ZIP archive

Unzip the downloaded folder directly into your 'saves' folder. Ensure the extracted folder directly contains 'level.dat' and the 'region' directory.

4

Step 4: Select Minecraft Java 1.19.2

In your Minecraft Launcher under 'Installations', make sure you launch Java Edition 1.19.2. Newer versions may cause world generation mismatches outside city boundaries.

5

Step 5: Launch and explore!

Start Minecraft, click 'Singleplayer', and select Brno in Minecraft from your world list. Have fun exploring virtual Brno!
