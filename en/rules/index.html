Server Rules (Historical Archive)

The original community guidelines enforced during the Brno in Minecraft public server operation.

"Please don't be a goofball. Treat others better than the way you want to be treated. <3"

These rules are preserved for historical and archival documentation.

1 Treat others with respect

Do not intentionally upset or provoke other players. Trolling, bullying, hate speech, and unprovoked attacks against players with PvP disabled were prohibited. Respect player builds and public infrastructure.

2 Avoid offensive content

Vulgarities, hate symbols, adult imagery, and offensive skins or structures were strictly disallowed. A welcoming environment for visitors of all ages was maintained.

3 Cheating is not tolerated

Hacks, bots, autoclickers, and resource packs giving unfair advantages (such as X-Ray) were forbidden. Quality-of-life additions like minimaps and damage indicators were permitted.

4 Keep chat family-friendly

Conversations were kept accessible and friendly for younger audiences, avoiding explicit or inappropriate topics.

5 Advertising and spam prohibited

Unsolicited promotions for other servers, external commercial projects, or repetitive command spam were strictly disallowed.

6 Do not exploit bugs

Players finding exploits or technical glitches were required to report them via GitHub issues rather than abusing them.
